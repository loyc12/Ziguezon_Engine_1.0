# Resource Subtypes

Resources should be grouped by category. Non-MVP entries can stay as
commented-out enum values until the resource system stabilizes.

Capacity resources are special: they are availability-priced,
non-transportable, and often behave like current `WORK`.

## Capacity

- area
- housing
- labour  ( split into subtypes ? )
- compute
- energy
- research

## Material

- organics

- volatiles
- sillicates  ( includes regolith )
- base metals
- rare earths
- deuterium

## Manufactured

- ingots
- plating
- fabrics
- structures
- electronics
- machinery

## Consumables

- appliances
- propellant

- dirty water
